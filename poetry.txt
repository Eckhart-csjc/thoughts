Writing a poem is like pulling teeth
Best do it quickly, if at all.
Some folks apply an anaesthetic
Drugs, delusions, alcohol
I'll admit to having tried more than one
But now for me the pain is half the fun

Writing a poem is like taking a shower
Scrubbing off the layers of grime
To get to the bare naked you
It isn't something you want to do 
In front of others... most of the time

Again the kingdom of poetry
Is like a sourdough yeast
First you have to feed it
To see if it awakes
If it does, then knead it
If it doesn't... pancakes!
